## Title: The Hobbit

Bilbo Baggins is a quiet hobbit who joins a group of dwarves on an unexpected adventure.
Themes: friendship, courage, fantasy, magic.

## Title: 1984

A dystopian novel about surveillance and totalitarian control.
Themes: freedom, surveillance, politics.

## Title: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

A young boy discovers he is a wizard and begins studying at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
Themes: magic, friendship, adventure, courage.

## Title: The Lord of the Rings

Frodo Baggins begins a dangerous journey to destroy the One Ring and save Middle-earth.
Themes: friendship, sacrifice, fantasy, heroism.

## Title: The Little Prince

A pilot meets a mysterious young prince who teaches important lessons about love and life.
Themes: friendship, love, philosophy, childhood.

## Title: The Alchemist

A shepherd named Santiago follows his dream of finding treasure while discovering his true destiny.
Themes: dreams, destiny, adventure, self-discovery.

## Title: Fahrenheit 451

A firefighter questions a society where books are forbidden and critical thinking is discouraged.
Themes: censorship, freedom, knowledge, society.

## Title: Brave New World

A futuristic society controls people through technology, conditioning and pleasure.
Themes: control, technology, freedom, society.

## Title: The Book Thief

A young girl in Nazi Germany discovers the power of books during World War II.
Themes: war, family, friendship, hope.

## Title: All Quiet on the Western Front

A German soldier experiences the brutal reality of World War I.
Themes: war, survival, friendship, tragedy.

## Title: Pride and Prejudice

Elizabeth Bennet navigates love, family expectations and social class in nineteenth-century England.
Themes: love, society, family, prejudice.

## Title: To Kill a Mockingbird

A young girl witnesses racism and injustice through her father's work as a lawyer.
Themes: justice, racism, family, courage.
